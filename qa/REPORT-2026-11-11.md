Target: live demo `https://merci-demo.myshopify.com/` (theme #161508261959). The user chose no staging store, so the tests only read pages and use the browser-session cart.

## Fixed in this phase

| Severity | Issue | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 | Images in `mc-*` sections had **no `alt` attribute**. `alt: x \| default: y` inside `image_tag` applies the filters to the whole tag | alt computed first (`assign mc_alt`) in 7 sections, with fallbacks |
| 🔴 | Touch screens could not add to the wishlist from a card (the hover strip was hidden) | round ♡ in the image corner on `(hover: none)` |
| 🟠 | Newsletter popup still carried the Top Market look (script title, camera illustration, "$30 coupon") | Merci copy, heading font, white background, lookbook photo |
| 🟠 | Contrast: compare-at price `#9f9f9f` (2.65:1), accent text on white (3.66:1), payment icons at 80% opacity, PDP price, collection sidebar banner link without a name, brand strip not keyboard-scrollable | compare `#767676`; accent text uses `color_accent_ink` `#a95b55` (4.87:1); opacity removed; `sb_alt`; `tm-scroller` adds `tabindex=0` to tracks with nothing focusable |
| 🟡 | Active nav item on the dark bar | primary `#bf7069` on `#231e17` (4.52:1); the deeper ink only on the light homepage overlay |
